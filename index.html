RisePath: Wake Up Early

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

RisePath: Wake Up Early ("RisePath," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the RisePath mobile application ("the App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Data stored on your device

Most of your data — including your alarms, wake-up history, streaks, badges, missions, bedtime intentions, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. This information stays on your phone and is not transmitted to us.

Anonymous account identifier

When you first open the App, we generate an anonymous account identifier using Google Firebase Authentication. This is a random string that identifies your device to our services (for example, to link your subscription and preserve your progress). It contains no personal information — no name, email, phone number, or contact details. You are not required to sign in or create an account.

Data stored for the Buddies feature

If you choose to use the Buddies (accountability) feature, we store the following in our cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore) so that you and your connected buddies can see each other's progress:

  • A buddy code that identifies your account to friends you connect with
  • Your display name
  • Your wake-up status, streak count, and related morning stats
  • Nudges you send to or receive from buddies

You control whether to use this feature. If you do not add any buddies, this data is not shared.

Subscription information

If you purchase a RisePath subscription, the transaction is processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We do not collect or store your payment details — these are handled securely by Google Play.

RevenueCat receives:

  • Your anonymous account identifier (described above)
  • Your subscription status (active, expired, trial, cancelled)
  • Purchase history and receipts from Google Play
  • Your device platform (Android) and app version

This information is used only to unlock and manage your premium features. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash & error reporting

To find and fix bugs, RisePath uses Sentry, a crash- and error-reporting service. When the App crashes or hits an error, Sentry collects technical diagnostic information only, such as:

  • The error message and code stack trace
  • Your device type and operating system version
  • The app version and general error context (for example, which screen was active)

This data is used solely to diagnose and fix problems. We do not send any personal information to Sentry — no names, no buddy data, no wake-up or alarm records, and no advertising or tracking identifiers. Crash reporting is disabled during our own development and testing.

What We Do Not Collect

RisePath does not use any analytics, advertising, or behavioral-tracking tools. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Any personal information beyond what is described above

How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data described above solely to:

  • Provide the App's core alarm and mission features
  • Enable the Buddies accountability feature you choose to use
  • Manage your subscription and premium access

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Local data remains on your device. Buddies data and your anonymous account identifier are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which employs industry-standard security measures. Subscription information is stored by RevenueCat. Crash reports are stored by Sentry. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-party services

RisePath uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Firestore) — anonymous authentication and Buddies data storage. See: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. See: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. See: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting. See: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Each service has its own privacy policy which governs how they handle your information.

Permissions

The App requests certain device permissions to function:

  • Notifications and alarm permissions — to fire your alarms reliably
  • Camera — for photo and QR-scan missions (only when you use them)
  • Microphone — for voice-based missions (only when you use them)
  • Motion sensors — to detect steps and movement for physical missions
  • Battery optimization — to ensure alarms fire on time

These permissions are used only for the features described and are never used to track you.

Children's Privacy

RisePath is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time using the "Reset progress" option in the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

To request full deletion of your data — including your anonymous account identifier, Buddies data from our cloud database, and any subscription records — contact us at the email below with the subject line "Delete my data" and include your buddy code (found in the App's Buddies section). We will delete your data within 30 days.

Note: Google Play retains purchase records for tax and legal purposes according to their own policies.
