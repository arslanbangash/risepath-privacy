RisePath

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

RisePath ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the RisePath mobile application ("the App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Data stored on your device

Most of your data — including your alarms, wake-up history, streaks, badges, missions, bedtime intentions, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. This information stays on your phone and is not transmitted to us.

Data stored for the Buddies feature

If you choose to use the Buddies (accountability) feature, we store the following in our cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore) so that you and your connected buddies can see each other's progress:

  • A buddy code that identifies your account to friends you connect with
  • Your display name
  • Your wake-up status, streak count, and related morning stats
  • Nudges you send to or receive from buddies

You control whether to use this feature. If you do not add any buddies, this data is not shared.

Subscription information

If you purchase a RisePath subscription, the transaction is processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We do not collect or store your payment details — these are handled securely by Google Play. We receive only your subscription status (active, expired, trial) to unlock premium features.

What We Do Not Collect

RisePath does not use any analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
  • Any personal information beyond what is described above

How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data described above solely to:

  • Provide the App's core alarm and mission features
  • Enable the Buddies accountability feature you choose to use
  • Manage your subscription and premium access

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Local data remains on your device. Buddies data is stored in Google Firebase, which employs industry-standard security measures. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Permissions

The App requests certain device permissions to function:

  • Notifications and alarm permissions — to fire your alarms reliably
  • Camera — for photo and QR-scan missions (only when you use them)
  • Microphone — for voice-based missions (only when you use them)
  • Motion sensors — to detect steps and movement for physical missions
  • Battery optimization — to ensure alarms fire on time

These permissions are used only for the features described and are never used to track you.

Children's Privacy

RisePath is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time using the "Reset progress" option in the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App. To request deletion of your Buddies data from our cloud database, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
